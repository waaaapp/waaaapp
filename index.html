BACKUP

Export and import

Save account names and photos in an encrypted workspace file. Import it on any supported computer, then link each account with your phone. Messages and login sessions are not included.

Keep this somewhere safe. It cannot be recovered.

Import adds accounts and keeps your current workspace intact.

PERMISSIONS

Camera and microphone

Set up microphone and camera access for WhatsApp calls. A quick test releases both devices immediately. If access is blocked, allow waaaapp in your operating system privacy settings and restart.

ABOUT

About waaaapp

waaaapp is an open-source app by Chris Ochieng, not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta.

Accounts open when you first select them and stay connected while the app is running. Unopened accounts do not receive notifications.

Switch accounts with ⌘/Ctrl + 1–9. Add one with ⌘/Ctrl + T. Open settings with ⌘/Ctrl + ,.
